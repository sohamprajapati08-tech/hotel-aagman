Who We Are

Your Peaceful Home Away from Home

Founded with a steadfast commitment to genuine care, Hotel Aagaman has grown into Kheralu's most trusted sanctuary for spiritual pilgrims, traveling executives, and vacationing families.

Located directly on Ambaji Highway at Vrundavan Circle, we combine modern comforts—like premium air conditioning, 24/7 hot water, high-speed Wi-Fi, and real-time online reservations—with the timeless warmth of Indian hospitality: "Atithi Devo Bhava".

Pure Dining Culture

100% Pure Desi Vegetarian Cuisine

Food at Hotel Aagaman is an experience of soul and devotion. Our kitchen uses only pure desi ghee, freshly ground spices, and handpicked local vegetables.

  • Authentic Kathiyawadi Gujarati Thali served with love
  • Rich North Indian Punjabi curries and hot tandoori naan
  • Crispy South Indian dosas and freshly churned masala chaas
  • Hygienic family dining hall and fast parcel takeaway service
